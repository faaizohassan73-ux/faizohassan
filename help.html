Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our tours? Check out our quick guide below for helpful information.

Question: Can I change my destination after I register?

Answer: Yes! You can request updates to your booking by contacting our team at least 14 days prior to departure.

Question: Is flight pricing included in the tour packages?

Answer: Tour rates cover hotel stays, city ground transport, and guided excursions. Flights can be added upon request.

Question: Do I need a visa before booking?

Answer: You can register for a trip anytime. However, ensure your travel visa is processed prior to your departure date.

Need further assistance? Connect with our dedicated support team through our social channels or drop us an email.
